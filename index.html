Our proposed solution

Meet the idea behind SafeConnect

We want to explore whether nearby compatible devices could help relay emergency messages when regular networks are unavailable.

🆘

SOS alerts

A simple way for a user to signal that they need emergency assistance.

🧭

Location sharing

A concept for sharing location information with trusted contacts or nearby devices.

📡

Nearby-device relay

Explore how compatible devices might relay alerts beyond a single device's connection.

SafeConnect is currently a startup concept, not a working emergency service. Feasibility, reliability, security, and privacy would need to be developed and tested. It is not a replacement for official emergency services.

A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

What is SafeConnect?

SafeConnect is an early-stage startup idea exploring offline emergency communication using nearby compatible devices.

Is SafeConnect available to use today?

No. It is currently a concept. A prototype would need to be developed and carefully tested before any real-world use.

Who is SafeConnect intended for?

We are initially exploring the needs of students, travellers, and communities that may experience unreliable connectivity.

Will it replace emergency services?

No. Any future version would be intended as a potential additional communication option, not a replacement for official emergency services.
